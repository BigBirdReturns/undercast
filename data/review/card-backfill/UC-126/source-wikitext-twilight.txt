'''Twilight Sparkle''' is the central main character of ''[[My Little Pony Friendship is Magic]]''. She is a female [[Unicorns|unicorn]] [[Ponies|pony]] who transforms into an [[Alicorns|Alicorn]] and becomes a princess in [[Magical Mystery Cure]]. She is also the daughter of [[Twilight Velvet and Night Light]], the younger sister of [[Shining Armor]], sister-in-law to [[Princess Cadance]], and paternal aunt to [[Flurry Heart]].

At the beginning of the series, Twilight and [[Spike]] move from their residence in [[Canterlot]] to the [[Golden Oak Library]] in [[Ponyville]] to study the [[magic]] of friendship, and in [[Twilight's Kingdom - Part 2]], she gains her own [[Castle of Friendship|castle]] along with the title "Princess of Friendship". She represents the [[Elements of Harmony#Twilight Sparkle – magic|element of magic]].

Before becoming a princess, Twilight regularly sends [[Friendship lessons|friendship reports]] to [[Princess Celestia]]. In [[Owl's Well That Ends Well]], she adopts a pet owl named [[Owlowiscious]]. As of [[The Last Problem]], she is the ruler of [[Equestria]].

Twilight's human counterpart, '''[[Twilight Sparkle (Sci-Twi)|Sci-Twi]]''', briefly serves as an antagonist in ''[[My Little Pony Equestria Girls: Friendship Games]]''.__TOC__{{clr}}

==Development and design==
[[File:Star Gazing Twilight Sketch.jpg|thumb|"Star Gazing Twilight", a sketch for the [[My Little Pony Friendship is Magic|series]]' original "show bible".]][[File:Lauren Faust G1 Twilight toy.jpg|thumb|[[Lauren Faust]]'s childhood Twilight toy.]] [[Lauren Faust]] was inspired to create Twilight Sparkle by the [[mlw:Twilight|G1 pony Twilight]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://twitter.com/Fyre_flye/status/406583985846763520|author=Lauren Faust|title=Twilight became Twilight|work=Twitter|date=2013-11-29|accessdate=2013-12-02}}</ref>  In the [[Lauren Faust/Gallery|preliminary character designs]] posted by Faust, Twilight Sparkle originally had the same color scheme and cutie mark as G1 Twilight. There is an [https://mlp.fandom.com/wiki/File:Twilight_Twinkle_concept_illustration.png intermittent design] based on the G3 [[Earth ponies|earth pony]] [[mlw:Twilight Twinkle|Twilight Twinkle]], with the same name and color scheme.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://twitter.com/Fyre_flye/status/312635999513608192|title=Lauren Faust via Twitter|accessdate=2013-03-15}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2_wqXelDV5o&t=6m10s|title=Interview with M.A. Larson|work=Midwestria 2012|date=2012-09-22|accessdate=2012-10-01}}</ref> This design has a cutie mark based on [[mlw:G1_Moondancer|G1 Moondancer]]. In her final design, Twilight Sparkle's color scheme, cutie mark, and name are more loosely adapted from Twilight Twinkle. She is also initially a [[Unicorns|unicorn]] like G1 Twilight.

Twilight Sparkle is the first to use the naming pattern later followed by [[Sunset Shimmer]], [[Starlight Glimmer]], and [[mlw:Starbeam Twinkle|Starbeam Twinkle]]. As an Alicorn with the title Princess Twilight Sparkle, her name is similar to that of [[mlw:Princess Sparkle|G1 Princess Sparkle]]. [[Moon Dancer]] refers to Twilight Sparkle as Twilight Twinkle once in [[Amending Fences]]. The G3 name is also used for one of Twilight Sparkle's outfits in Gameloft's [[My Little Pony (mobile game)|mobile game]].

Lauren Faust had planned for Twilight to acquire the power of self-levitation "at some point."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://comments.deviantart.com/1/304960264/2577437132|title=Comment on Help the Bronycon Documentary|author=Lauren Faust|date=2012-06-04|accessdate=2012-11-11}}</ref> Following Faust's departure from the show, Twilight has used this power in the season three episodes [[The Crystal Empire - Part 2]] and [[Magical Mystery Cure]].

As an Alicorn, Princess Twilight Sparkle is a little taller,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://comments.deviantart.com/4/1453017/2947592321|title=DeviantArt: Comment on *Sibsy's profile|author=Sabrina Alberghetti|date=2013-02-18|accessdate=2013-12-13|quote=Yeah, she's a little taller.}}</ref> her neck is slightly more curved, and her horn is a bit longer than as a unicorn. In the future shown in [[The Last Problem]], she has grown a lot taller and has a similar design to [[Princess Celestia]], as well as a flowing mane and tail like Celestia and [[Princess Luna]]'s.

Concurrently with G4, a G1 version of either Twilight Sparkle or [[List of ponies#S02E26 UUM12|S02E26 Unnamed Unicorn Mare #12]] has been introduced [http://tfwiki.net/wiki/File:Pony_Megatron_Taste_Test.jpg in] [[IDW comics|IDW]] Publishing's [http://tfwiki.net/wiki/The_War_Never_Ends ''Transformers vs. G.I. Joe'' Issue #13].

==Depiction in the series==
===History===
[[File:Twilight asks Spike to look for "Predictions and Prophecies" book S1E01.png|thumb|left|Twilight's original home in [[Canterlot]].]] Twilight Sparkle lives in an ivory tower in [[Canterlot]] until [[Princess Celestia]] sends her to [[Ponyville]]. Accompanied by [[Spike]], Twilight checks the town's preparations for the Summer Sun Celebration and meets the rest of the [[Characters#Main characters|main characters]]. The celebration is interrupted when [[Princess Luna#Nightmare Moon|Nightmare Moon]], Celestia's nemesis, returns to power and threatens to bring eternal night to [[Equestria]]. Twilight and the rest of the Mane Six discover the [[Elements of Harmony]] and use them to transform Nightmare Moon back into [[Princess Luna]]. Princess Celestia gives Twilight permission to remain in Ponyville to study the [[magic]] of friendship.

Throughout the series, Twilight learns about the trials and rewards of friendship, periodically sending [[Friendship lessons|friendship reports]] to Princess Celestia. On rare occasions, Twilight and her friends work together to defeat powerful villains such as [[Discord]], [[Queen Chrysalis]], [[King Sombra]], and [[Lord Tirek]].

In [[Magical Mystery Cure]], Twilight completes an ancient unfinished magic spell with her deep understanding of friendship. Princess Celestia tells Twilight that she is ready for the next stage of her life, and Twilight transforms into an [[Alicorns|Alicorn]] and gains the title of princess.

Princess Twilight Sparkle becomes the "Princess of Friendship" in the season four episode [[Twilight's Kingdom - Part 2]]. In the season nine episode [[The Last Problem]], she is crowned the new ruler of Equestria, taking Celestia and Luna's place when they retire.

====Cutie mark====
[[File:Examiners Examining Filly Twilight S1E23.png|thumb|Twilight feeling nervous for her entrance exam.]] Twilight recounts how she got her [[Cutie marks|cutie mark]] in [[The Cutie Mark Chronicles]]. She studied magic with great interest as a filly and sought to enroll in [[Princess Celestia]]'s [[School for Gifted Unicorns]], though her entrance exam nearly ended in disaster when she failed to magically hatch a dragon egg as requested. A moment after giving up, the shockwave from a distant [[Sonic rainboom (event)|explosion]] caused Twilight's magic to surge out of control and wreak havoc around the exam room. After Princess Celestia showed up and diffused the situation, Twilight regained her senses and discovered that her cutie mark had appeared.

===Personality===
====Sociability and friendship====
Twilight is asocial when initially introduced in the show; at the beginning of the [[Friendship is Magic, part 1|first episode]], she hastily excuses herself when invited to a [[Moon Dancer|friend]]'s get-together so she can study in her room in [[Canterlot]]. She later agrees to oversee preparations for the [[Princess Celestia#Summer Sun Celebration|Summer Sun Celebration]] in [[Ponyville]] but is dismayed when [[Princess Celestia]] also assigns her to "make some friends". Twilight balks at every offer for friendship while checking on preparations, though in the [[Friendship is Magic, part 2|second part]] of the premiere, she becomes friends with the rest of the [[Characters#Generation 4|Mane Six]] and expresses desire to stay with them in Ponyville even after her task there is complete.

In [[Amending Fences]], Twilight returns to Canterlot and attempts to apologize to her former friends for her past asocial behavior. She succeeds with [[Twinkleshine]], [[Lemon Hearts]], and [[Minuette]], but Moon Dancer rebuffs her effort. Minuette and Twilight liken Moon Dancer's behavior to the way Twilight used to act.

Twilight is very close with the rest of the Mane Six and Spike, and she is forgiving enough to lend a hand to those she considers worthy of second chances, such as [[Starlight Glimmer]].

====Love of books and knowledge====
[[File:Twilight Studying S1E4.png|thumb|left|Twilight Sparkle reading a book on the library balcony.]] For the first four seasons of the show, Twilight resides in the [[Golden Oak Library]] in Ponyville and is regularly shown reading and organizing its books. She becomes enraptured upon seeing the large volume of books in the [[Crystal Empire]]'s library in [[The Crystal Empire - Part 1]], and in [[My Little Pony Equestria Girls: Forgotten Friendship]], Twilight nearly passes out when she learns that the Canterlot Library has a restricted section. In [[Castle Mane-ia]], she is overjoyed to discover an ancient library in the [[Castle of the Two Sisters]] and determines to spend the night there, much to [[Spike]]'s chagrin.

Unsurprisingly, Twilight demonstrates intellectual curiosity and textbook knowledge on a wide range of topics throughout the series. In [[Fall Weather Friends]], she cites books on running to explain how she earned fifth place in a marathon without prior racing experience. When [[Trixie]] claims to have defeated an [[Creatures#Ursa|Ursa Major]] in [[Boast Busters]], Twilight becomes intrigued and researches the creature in the library. In [[Luna Eclipsed]], [[Testing Testing 1, 2, 3]], [[The Lost Treasure of Griffonstone]], and other episodes, Twilight shows off a detailed knowledge of the history of [[Equestria]]. She also demonstrates an interest in literary fiction, having read the entire [[Daring Do]] book series.

====Rationality and overreaction====
Twilight strives to be rational even in difficult or unfamiliar situations. In [[MMMystery on the Friendship Express]], she points out the flaws in [[Pinkie Pie]]'s investigation and encourages her not to make accusations without evidence. When Twilight's "failsafe" spell fails to abate the bizarre phenomena plaguing [[Sweet Apple Acres]] in [[The Return of Harmony Part 1]], she assigns her friends a set of physical tasks that address the immediate issues.

[[File:Twilight tries to measure Pinkie's predictions S1E15.png|thumb|Twilight attempts to observe and measure Pinkie Sense omens.]] Twilight tends to be skeptical of supernatural claims, such as her friends' belief that [[Zecora]] is an evil enchantress in [[Bridle Gossip]], as well as Pinkie's purported ability to [[Pinkie Pie#Pinkie Sense|sense events in the immediate future]] in [[Feeling Pinkie Keen]]. In [[Castle Mane-ia]], when the sound of a church organ echoes through the halls of the [[Castle of the Two Sisters]], Twilight dismisses concerns that it could be the "Pony of Shadows" and leads her friends to investigate the source.

Twilight's logical thinking sometimes loses touch with reality. As the [[Parasprites|parasprite]] horde consumes Ponyville in [[Swarm of the Century]], Twilight suggests building a replica of the town nearby in anticipation of [[Princess Celestia]]'s imminent arrival. In [[Lesson Zero]], Twilight convinces herself that failing to send a [[Friendship lessons|friendship report]] by the end of the day will get her sent back to "magic kindergarten". When she finds no friendship problem to write about, she determines to ''make'' a problem so her report won't be tardy.

In ''[[My Little Pony The Movie]]'', Twilight rationalizes that not every problem can be solved with friendship outside of Equestria in light of the difficult experiences they face during their search for [[the Storm King]]. In the underwater kingdom of [[Seaquestria]], Twilight lets the rest of the Mane Six act friendly toward the [[Seaponies and mermares|seaponies]] to create a distraction while she attempts to steal a powerful artifact. The operation fails, however, eventually costing the ponies their friendship and leading Twilight to get captured by [[Tempest Shadow]].

In later seasons, Twilight develops a tendency to overreact in what her friends refer to as "Twilight-ing". In [[The Beginning of the End - Part 1]], she hyperventilates after being told that she will become ruler of Equestria after Celestia and Luna retire. In [[The Summer Sun Setback]], she puts her whole craziness aside in order to make preparations for the final Summer Sun Celebration and her future role as ruler of Equestria. In [[A Trivial Pursuit]], she is so desperate to win a trivia competition that she overrides Pinkie's enjoyment of the game.

Twilight appears terrified when Spike claims there's a mouse behind her in [[It's About Time]]. She admits to an irrational fear of quesadillas in [[Party Pooped]], and in [[Starlight the Hypnotist]], Twilight seeks help for an irrational fear of ladybugs that reportedly developed in her youth.

===Skills===
====Organization====
Twilight demonstrates strong organizational skills throughout the series. In [[Winter Wrap Up]], she delegates responsibilities to the residents of [[Ponyville]] so that they finish their [[Winter Wrap Up (event)|seasonal cleaning effort]] on time, leading them to name her the "All-Team Organizer". During the cider-making contest in [[The Super Speedy Cider Squeezy 6000]], Twilight assigns tasks to [[Pinkie Pie]], [[Rarity]], [[Fluttershy]], and [[Rainbow Dash]] to help raise the [[Apple family]]'s rate of production, leading to a five-fold increase.

[[File:Twilight devilish look 4 S2E10.png|thumb|left|If this spoon does not rest precisely on the edge of the bowl, everything is ruined.]] In the book ''[[The Art of Equestria]]'', then-supervising director [[Jayson Thiessen]] suggests that Twilight has "a touch of OCD". She takes pains to ensure precise positioning of a ladle in a punch bowl in [[Secret of My Excess]], and in [[Spike at Your Service]], Twilight's intense reading focus is broken when [[Applejack]] slightly shifts an inkwell on her desk. In [[It's About Time]], a distressed Twilight is kept awake all night by the fact that her schedule for the upcoming month doesn't leave time to schedule the next month.{{clr}}

====Teaching====
[[File:Twilight "see you next time" S4E15.png|thumb|Twilight teaching the Cutie Mark Crusaders.]] In the episode [[Twilight Time]], Twilight takes time each week to teach the [[Cutie Mark Crusaders]] various skills. She attempts to teach [[Sweetie Belle]] to use the telekinetic component of her magic, [[Apple Bloom]] how to make botanic growth potions, and [[Scootaloo]] how to strip and reassemble various types of scooter and cycle. Twilight is overjoyed when she thinks the other classmates also want to learn new things, but she is disappointed when she finds out that they merely want to spend time with a local celebrity.

Twilight's ability as a teacher is proven in that she succeeds in teaching all three Crusaders the skills she intended to teach them. They all demonstrate a high degree of aptitude at the end of their lessons.

Twilight attempts to teach Rainbow Dash the history of the [[Wonderbolts]], but her methods of study prove futile when Rainbow Dash acts as a class clown in [[Testing Testing 1, 2, 3]]. In [[The Cutie Re-Mark - Part 2]], Twilight takes [[Starlight Glimmer]] on as a student, and she attempts to give Starlight her first friendship lesson in [[The Crystalling - Part 1]] by sending her to reunite with [[Sunburst]]. In the end, Twilight laments about her alleged failure, but Spike renews her resolve.

In the [[School Daze - Part 1|season eight]] [[School Daze - Part 2|premiere]], Twilight opens the [[School of Friendship]] in order to teach the magic of friendship to all races and spread it across Equestria and beyond. She serves as the school's headmistress, and she and her friends all serve as teachers. In [[A Horse Shoe-In]], however, she gives both of her roles to Starlight when being assigned to take [[Princess Celestia]] and [[Princess Luna]]'s places as the future leader of [[Equestria]].

In [[The Last Problem]], after being crowned as leader of Equestria, Twilight is put in charge of the [[School for Gifted Unicorns]], and helps her future student [[Luster Dawn]] realize the importance of friendship in a similar way to learning it herself from Celestia.{{clr}}

====[[Spike]]====
[[File:Spike tearfully hugging Twilight S9E24.png|thumb|Twilight hugging Spike.]] Twilight's adopted younger brother as stated in [[Sparkle's Seven]]. Throughout the series, Spike serves as Twilight's number-one assistant, sending her letters to [[Princess Celestia]] for her [[friendship lessons]]. In Sparkle's Seven, Spike feels left out of the "Sibling Supreme" competition and tells Fluttershy how he always saw Twilight and Shining Armor as his siblings. At the end of the episode, Shining Armor and Twilight give Spike the "Sibling Supreme" crown as they both consider Spike to be "the little brother they always had".{{clr}}

===Crowns, etc.===
For the first three seasons of the show, Twilight possesses a crown containing the [[Elements of Harmony#Twilight Sparkle – magic|element of magic]]. She receives it in [[Friendship is Magic, part 2]] when she defeats [[Princess Luna#Nightmare Moon|Nightmare Moon]]. The crown is studded with sapphires and a magenta star-shaped gem shaped like Twilight's cutie mark.

After Twilight becomes a princess in [[Magical Mystery Cure]], she wears a different crown with the element of magic embedded in it. It is a lighter shade of gold, no longer has sapphires, and rests horizontally, not vertically, on Twilight's head. It appears again in [[Shadow Play - Part 2]] and [[The Beginning of the End - Part 1]].

Twilight relinquishes her crown when she and her friends return the [[Elements of Harmony]] to the [[Tree of Harmony]] in [[Princess Twilight Sparkle - Part 2]]. At the [[Summer Sun Celebration]], she wears a new crown, which has the same shade of gold but looks completely different from her element of magic crown. It has more of a "classic" crown shape, with each point studded with a small magenta jewel. It becomes her regular crown from then onward, as seen in [[Trade Ya!]], [[Equestria Games]], [[Twilight's Kingdom - Part 1]], and [[Princess Spike]].

In ''[[My Little Pony The Movie]]'', Twilight wears a somewhat different crown. This crown is destroyed when [[Tempest Shadow]] stomps on it.

In [[The Last Problem]], when Twilight becomes the new ruler of [[Equestria]], she receives [[Princess Celestia]] and [[Princess Luna]]'s merged crown.

==Depiction in ''Equestria Girls''==
===''[[My Little Pony Equestria Girls]]''===
[[File:Twilight and friends arm in arm EG.png|thumb|Twilight and her friends in Equestria Girls.]] In ''My Little Pony Equestria Girls'', Twilight and Spike teleport to a human world to retrieve the stolen [[Elements of Harmony|element of magic]] from [[Sunset Shimmer]]. Twilight and Spike enter [[Canterlot High School|Canterlot High]] and meet the human counterparts of their Ponyville friends. When Twilight discovers that [[Principal Celestia]] will award the crown to the Princess of the Fall Formal dance, she decides to run for Princess. She wins, despite a smear campaign orchestrated by Sunset Shimmer, and receives the crown at the dance.

However, Sunset Shimmer takes the crown by force and transforms into a rage-filled [[Creatures#Demon|demon]]. She hypnotizes the students, whom she plans to use as an army to invade Equestria. When she attacks Twilight, Twilight's friends shield her and inadvertently activate the magic of friendship. They defeat Sunset Shimmer, who is remorseful for her actions.

After the dance, Twilight and Spike return to Equestria. Twilight feels more confident about being a princess than before. She bumps into [[Flash Sentry]], who she encountered several times in the human world, and blushes. Twilight's friends tease her for having a crush, but she denies it.

===''[[My Little Pony Equestria Girls: Rainbow Rocks|Equestria Girls: Rainbow Rocks]]''===
In the film, the reformed Sunset Shimmer uses a magical book to write a note to Princess Celestia that magically appears in a book in Celestia's library. When Twilight sees this book, she senses that her CHS friends need her help, so she and Spike go through a modified version of the [[Crystal Mirror]] to return to the human world.

Once there, Twilight is told of the situation at CHS while also revealing her new status back home in Equestria as the Princess of Friendship. Twilight learns that her human friends are still able to assume half-pony forms after she returned home with the Element of Magic. She attempts to take out [[the Dazzlings]] at the pre-Battle of the Bands party immediately, only to find the magic of friendship not working. Believing that they need a counter-spell song to counteract the Dazzlings' song, Twilight struggles to come up with one as [[the Rainbooms]] battle their way to the finals to buy Twilight more time.

When they are dropped into the basement of the amphitheater stage by [[Trixie and the Illusions]], under encouragement from the Dazzlings, Twilight gives up hope, only to discover that the reason the magic of friendship never worked was because the Rainbooms had allowed a few disagreements to drive a rift into their friendships. That caused the magic of friendship to become warped into something the Dazzlings could feed upon and render it powerless.

[[File:The Rainbooms "Be afraid of the dark" EG2.png|thumb|left|Twilight Sparkle leading the Rainbooms in ''Rainbow Rocks''.]] Once the Rainbooms realize their error and make amends, together with [[DJ Pon-3 (EG)|DJ Pon-3]], who was immune to the Dazzlings' spell thanks to her headphones, and Sunset lending her strength to them, Twilight and the band are able to overpower and destroy the Dazzlings' power sources, stripping them of their dark powers permanently, and leaving them to be forced off the stage in disgrace by the angry students and faculty.

Afterward, Twilight returns home to Equestria, but thanks to how she was able to re-energize the portal, she can come back to visit anytime she wants. Sunset also takes it upon herself to write her friendship reports into her old journal for Twilight to read back in Equestria.

After the credits roll, the human world's Twilight pieces together some strange events that occurred at Canterlot High School. She talks to her non-sentient dog Spike. This version of Twilight looks extremely different as she has her hair in a bun and wears thick black glasses, similar to how pony Twilight is dressed when she plays a therapist to Rainbow Dash in [[Lesson Zero]].

====[[Rainbow Rocks animated shorts|''Rainbow Rocks'' animated shorts]]====
Twilight appears in her human form in [[Shake Your Tail]], where she helps her friends come up with a theme for a dance at Canterlot High. She comes up with a classroom theme, but that idea does not sit well with the others. She later performs at the dance with the Rainbooms. In [[Perfect Day for Fun]], she spends some time with [[Fluttershy (EG)|Fluttershy]] at the Canterlot High carnival. At one point, she gives Fluttershy a [[Discord]] plushie, but Fluttershy smacks it with a Whack-a-Mole hammer. She and Fluttershy spend most of the short trying to find the others. They finally reunite at the photo booth, taking some photos before leaving for the stage to perform. In one photo montage, Twilight and [[Applejack (EG)|Applejack]] drag a crying Fluttershy into a haunted house. However, Applejack and Twilight end up getting scared themselves while Fluttershy shows indifference towards it. The final scene shows Twilight and her friends looking through the photos on top of a ferris wheel. In the novelization, Twilight is actually still in Equestria and, in the end, imagines that she is performing with her Canterlot High friends.

She also appears in the encore shorts [[My Past is Not Today]] and [[Friendship Through the Ages]]. In the former, she briefly appears in a flashback offering her hand to Sunset Shimmer after her defeat. In the latter, she performs the titular song with the Rainbooms, singing the first verse on piano, and happily interacts with Sunset Shimmer.

==Depiction in ''My Little Pony The Movie''==
In ''[[My Little Pony The Movie]]'', Twilight organizes the Festival of Friendship in Canterlot. When the city is invaded by [[the Storm King]]'s forces, led by [[Tempest Shadow]], Twilight and her friends escape to seek out the "Queen of the Hippos"—later specified to be [[Queen Novo]] of the Hippogriffs—for help, as per Celestia's instructions. During their journey, they are nearly sold into slavery by [[Capper]] and thrown overboard [[Captain Celaeno]]'s airship. When they find Novo in [[Locations#Seaquestria|Seaquestria]] and she refuses to help save their home, Twilight resorts to attempting to steal her Pearl of Transformation, resulting in her and her friends' banishment from Seaquestria when she gets caught.

After a heated falling-out with her friends, Twilight is captured by Tempest and brought back to Canterlot, where the Storm King uses the Staff of Sacanas to drain her and the other princesses' magic. Twilight saves Tempest from being swept up by the Storm King's tornado when he betrays her, and Twilight's friends arrive to rescue her and liberate Canterlot. Twilight and her friends eventually succeed in defeating the Storm King, and she restores Tempest with the staff after she sacrifices herself to save them from his final attack. In the end, Canterlot is restored, the Friendship Festival resumes, and Twilight and her friends befriend Tempest.

==Depiction in comics==
[[File:Comic micro 1 page 1.png|thumb|112px|Twilight in {{comic|m1}}.]] In early [[IDW comics]], Twilight Sparkle appears as a [[Unicorns|unicorn]]. In {{comic|13}} onward, she is depicted as an [[Alicorns|Alicorn]] princess.

In [[The Return of Queen Chrysalis]], Twilight and her friends quell the [[changelings]]' invasion of Ponyville and rescue the [[Cutie Mark Crusaders]] from [[Queen Chrysalis]]. In [[Neigh Anything]] Twilight learns of how [[Princess Cadance]] and [[Shining Armor]] met and fell in love. In [[My Little Pirate: Friendship Ahoy]], she briefly serves as pirate captain [[Hoofbeard]]'s navigator. In the [[IDW comics sixth story arc]], she ventures into various books in an attempt to fix their stories.

In [[Reflections]], Twilight and her friends travel to another dimension and help a good [[King Sombra]] defeat evil versions of Celestia and Luna. In [[The Good, the Bad and the Ponies]], she helps Applejack save Canter Creek from [[King Longhorn]] and the Cattle Rustler Gang. [[File:Comic issue 19 Alternate Twilight.png|left|thumb|112px|An alternate universe's Twilight.]] In [[Night of the Living Apples]], Twilight and her friends stop an invading army of evil magically-sentient apples. In {{scomic|94}}, Twilight calls in Pinkie to organize the Festival of the Two Sisters, and invites Cheese Sandwich to join her. In {{scomic|95}}, she tries to keep the populace calm while Canterlot is magically stripped of sound.

In {{scomic|100}}, Twilight is researching the other Trees of Harmony alongside Starlight Glimmer, Star Swirl the Bearded, Sunburst and Stygian, when Rainbow Dash crashes into the castle library, and warns her that the Knights of Harmony are coming.

In {{scomic|101}}, Twilight oversees the defensive measures for the upcoming attack. She attempts to negotiate with the Knights' leader, King Danu, but is unable to dissuade him from his goal of destroying the Elements of Harmony. After the Knights' attack forces a retreat, Twilight is reminded by Mage Meadowbrook that she shared the power of the Elements of Harmony with several others, meaning that they are all targets. Twilight quickly realizes that this includes the Young Six, and she and her friends find them being held hostage by the Knights.

In {{scomic|102}}, Twilight is told of the Knights' history. When Discord and Trixie provide a diversion, Twilight and her allies split up their forces and defeat most of the Knights. However, she and Star Swirl are ambushed by Balor; he takes their magic and transfers it to Danu, who threatens to destroy Canterlot with it. The Element bearers from the other lands arrive to help and join with Twilight and her friends to defeat Danu. Afterwards, Twilight proposes an alliance of nations.
