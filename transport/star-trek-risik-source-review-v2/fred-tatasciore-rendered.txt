.pi-navigation{text-align:center;font-weight:normal} Fred Tatasciore Birth name: Fred Oliver Tatasciore Date of birth: 13 June 1968 Place of birth: New York City, New York, USA Roles: Voice actor Character(s): Lieutenant Shaxs ; Additional characters Fred Tatasciore ( born 13 June 1968 ; age 58) is a voice actor who voiced Lieutenant Shaxs on Star Trek: Lower Decks . He also voiced other characters on Lower Decks and Star Trek: Prodigy . Earlier, he lent his voice to Star Trek Into Darkness . Together with David Sobolov he provided all the Klingon voices heard in the film except the one of Sean Blakemore . Sobolov and Tatasciore were coached by Marc Okrand . Tatasciore is known to be the voice of the Hulk , the Beast , and Bane in several animated films and series and video games. He voiced James T. Kirk , Leonard McCoy , and Spock in several episodes of the television series Robot Chicken (2005-14). Tatasciore attended the UCLA and started as voice actor in the early 1980s when he worked on the English dubbed versions of the Hong Kong action films Dragon Lord (1982), Project A (1983), Born to Defence (1986), Swordsman II (1992), The Bodyguard from Beijing (1994), Fist of Legend (1994), and High Risk (1995). Further film work includes Team America: World Police (2004), Final Fantasy VII: Advent Children (2005), The Wild (2006, William Shatner , Richard Kind , Greg Cipes , David Cowgill , Kevin Michael Richardson , Nicholas Guest , Nolan North and Julianne Buescher ), and The Ant Bully (2006), Charlotte's Web (2006), Dead Silence (2007, with Bob Gunton ), TMNT (2007, with Patrick Stewart , Nolan North, Kevin Michael Richardson, and Barbara Harris ), Meet the Robinsons (2007), Enchanted (2007), Beowulf (2007, with Sharisse Baker-Bernard , Greg Ellis , Shay Duffin , and Dominic Keating ), Madagascar: Escape 2 Africa (2008), 9 (2009), The Princess and the Frog (2009), and Tangled (2010, with Donna Murphy ), Hellraiser: Revelations (2011), and Kung Fu Panda 2 (2011), Wreck-It Ralph (2012), Planes (2013), Frozen (2013), and Planes: Fire & Rescue (2014). On television, Tatasciore lent his voice to the series Naruto (2002-03), Star Wars: Clone Wars (2005), What's New, Scooby-Doo? (2003-05), Invader Zim (2001-06), Tim and Eric Awesome Show, Great Job! Mystery Incorporated (2010-13, with Frank Welker ), Mad (2010-13), Kung Fu Panda: Legends of Awesomeness (2011-14), Monsters vs. Aliens (2013-14), Mickey Mouse (2013-14), Ultimate Spider-Man (2012-15), American Dad (2005-15), Family Guy (1999-2015), Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (2013-15), Marvel's Avengers Assemble (2013-15), and Hulk and the Agents of S.M.A.S.H.
