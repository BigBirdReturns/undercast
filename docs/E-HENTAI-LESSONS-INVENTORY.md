# E-Hentai operating lessons inventory

**Recorded:** 2026-07-25
**Purpose:** maintain the useful lessons of a long-running community archive
without copying its content policy, incentive economy or infrastructure before
UnderCast demonstrates the same need.

E-Hentai is an operating reference, not a product template. UnderCast adopts a
mechanism only when a real archive path establishes the corresponding need.

## Inventory

| Lesson | UnderCast state | Maintenance disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Permanent object identity | Proven: stable `UC-*` routes, tombstones and successor redirects | Maintain |
| Immutable provenance | Proven: exact source revisions, SHA-256 manifests, BagIt snapshots and independent offsite verification | Maintain and run restore drills |
| Honest retirement | Proven: wrong bindings become absence while hashes, receipts and historical bytes remain | Expose richer public history later |
| Namespaced consensus | Proven internally: identity/presentation votes and weak, active, solid, enforced and contested states | Finish sitewide review; do not infer identity |
| Advanced discovery | Partial: shareable search and filters | Add exact, negative, OR, aliases and namespaced search only through the identity/discovery milestone |
| Duplicate and replacement control | Partial: exact hashes, cross-card invariants and bounded visual audits | Add canonical pre-intake similarity search after a real duplicate escapes |
| Replacement chains | Partial: tombstones and audit journals | Make correction and successor history publicly legible |
| Public corrections and appeals | Planned, not shipped | Build record-specific intake, append-only disposition and independent appeal before contributor accounts |
| Rights reporting | Partial preservation policy | Separate rights/legal intake from ordinary moderation; measure and drill the response path |
| Personal research memory | Intentionally absent server-side | Consider private local saved views, notes and watched/hidden facets before accounts |
| Low-resource access | Proven: no-JS records, keyboard access, mobile layouts and bounded initial loading | Maintain |
| Distributed delivery | Preservation copies exist; live serving remains centralized | Do not build an H@H analogue without measured bandwidth or availability failure |
| Load control | Partial: collection-only mode, bounded leases and waterline capacity | Complete operational SLO, rollback and incident work |
| Public data interface | Partial: static archive contract, schemas, shards and JSON-LD | Stabilize versioned exports before demand-backed read-only APIs |
| Community maintenance | Internal reviewer roles only | Introduce bounded proposers only after correction capacity and governance are proven |
| Reputation and incentives | Deliberately not authoritative | Do not adopt automatic reputation acceptance or volume leaderboards |
| Continuity and succession | Partial: offsite snapshot and canonical gate | Prove restore, rollback, owner-unavailable and multiple-steward drills |
| Feature restraint | Proven: constitution, decision ledger, anti-goals and collection-only default | Maintain an explicit refusal boundary |

## Priority

### P0 — current maintenance

1. Complete the operational-reliability milestone: measured SLOs, fresh restore,
   bad-publication rollback, incident receipts and rights-response drill.
2. Preserve the sitewide media denominator and work down its review debt without
   converting uncertainty into acceptance.
3. Keep replacement, correction and absence history durable.

### P1 — after operational reliability

1. Public record-specific correction and rights intake.
2. Legible replacement chains and correction history.
3. More expressive, accessible discovery.
4. Versioned static exports followed by demand-backed read-only interfaces.

### P2 — demonstrated demand only

1. Private/local collections, notes and watched facets.
2. Bounded contributor proposals and independent appeals.
3. Distributed serving or cache nodes only after measured CDN or bandwidth
   pressure establishes the requirement.

## Mechanisms to learn from

- [Gallery tagging](https://ehwiki.org/wiki/Format): namespaces, visible claim
  strength, vote history and fallible consensus.
- [Gallery searching](https://ehwiki.org/wiki/searches): exact, negative, OR,
  namespaced and file-similarity discovery.
- [Gallery chains](https://ehwiki.org/wiki/chain): durable replacement lineage
  and preservation of accumulated metadata.
- [Expunging](https://ehwiki.org/wiki/Expunging): petitions, reversibility and
  structurally separate appeals.
- [Reporting](https://ehwiki.org/wiki/Reporting): distinct legal, safety and
  technical complaint paths.
- [Favorites](https://ehwiki.org/wiki/Favorites): long-term private research
  organization and searchable notes.
- [Hentai@Home](https://ehwiki.org/wiki/Hentai%40Home): hash verification,
  resumable queues, health-based traffic and fallback priorities.
- [API](https://ehwiki.org/wiki/API): explicit batching and load limits.
- [Never To Be Added](https://ehwiki.org/wiki/Never_To_Be_Added): refusal and
  operational restraint are part of maintaining a long-lived system.

## Explicit non-adoptions

UnderCast does not inherit E-Hentai's content policy, economy or automatic
authority model. In particular:

- traffic does not determine canonical truth;
- points, donations or hosting capacity do not purchase review authority;
- model confidence and reputation do not directly accept facts;
- open contribution does not imply canonical write access;
- mature-looking infrastructure is not built before a real operating failure
  justifies it.
