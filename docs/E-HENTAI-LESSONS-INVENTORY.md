# E-Hentai operating lessons inventory

**Original checkpoint:** 2026-07-25

**Current successor:** `agent/estate-consolidation-001` / PR #132

**Purpose:** retain useful operating lessons from a long-running community archive without copying its content policy, incentive economy, or infrastructure before UnderCast demonstrates the same need.

E-Hentai is an operating reference, not a product template. UnderCast adopts a mechanism only when a real archive path establishes the corresponding need.

## Inventory

| Lesson | UnderCast state | Maintenance disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Permanent object identity | Proven through stable `UC-*` routes, tombstones, and successor redirects | Maintain |
| Immutable provenance | Proven through exact source revisions, SHA-256 manifests, snapshots, and verification | Maintain and drill restores |
| Honest retirement | Proven when wrong bindings become absence while hashes and receipts remain | Make public history more legible |
| Namespaced consensus | Proven internally through identity and presentation rulings | Finish sitewide review without inferring identity |
| Advanced discovery | Partial through shareable search and filters | Add exact, negative, OR, alias, and namespaced search only through a governed milestone |
| Duplicate and replacement control | Partial through exact hashes and cross-card invariants | Add pre-intake similarity search after a measured escape |
| Replacement chains | Partial through tombstones and audit journals | Expose correction and successor lineage |
| Public corrections and appeals | Planned | Build record-specific intake and independent appeal before contributor accounts |
| Rights reporting | Partial | Keep rights and legal intake separate from ordinary moderation and drill the response path |
| Personal research memory | Intentionally absent server-side | Prefer private local saved views and notes before accounts |
| Low-resource access | Proven through no-JS records, keyboard access, mobile layouts, and bounded initial loading | Maintain |
| Distributed delivery | Preservation copies exist; live serving is centralized | Do not build distributed serving without measured availability or bandwidth failure |
| Load control | Partial through collection-only mode, bounded leases, and capacity controls | Complete SLO, rollback, and incident work |
| Public data interface | Partial through static contracts, schemas, shards, and JSON-LD | Stabilize versioned exports before demand-backed APIs |
| Community maintenance | Internal reviewer roles only | Introduce bounded proposers only after correction capacity and governance are proven |
| Reputation and incentives | Deliberately non-authoritative | Do not allow reputation or volume to accept facts |
| Continuity and succession | Partial through offsite snapshots and the canonical gate | Prove restore, rollback, owner-unavailable, and multiple-steward drills |
| Feature restraint | Proven through constitutions, decision ledgers, anti-goals, and collection-only defaults | Maintain refusal boundaries |

## Priority

### P0: current maintenance

1. Complete operational reliability with measured SLOs, a fresh restore, bad-publication rollback, incident receipts, and a rights-response drill.
2. Preserve the sitewide media denominator and reduce review debt without converting uncertainty into acceptance.
3. Keep replacement, correction, and absence history durable.
4. Convert branch-local evidence into canonical, integrated, deployed, and maintained estate value.

### P1: after operational reliability

1. Public record-specific correction and rights intake.
2. Legible replacement chains and correction history.
3. More expressive accessible discovery.
4. Versioned static exports followed by demand-backed read-only interfaces.

### P2: demonstrated demand only

1. Private local collections, notes, and watched facets.
2. Bounded contributor proposals and independent appeals.
3. Distributed serving only after measured CDN or bandwidth pressure establishes the requirement.

## Explicit non-adoptions

UnderCast does not inherit E-Hentai's content policy, economy, or automatic authority model. In particular:

- traffic does not determine canonical truth;
- points, donations, or hosting capacity do not purchase review authority;
- model confidence and reputation do not directly accept facts;
- open contribution does not imply canonical write access;
- mature-looking infrastructure is not built before a real operating failure justifies it.
