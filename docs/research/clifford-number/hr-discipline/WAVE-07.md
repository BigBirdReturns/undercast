# Clifford Number HR disciplinary-process Wave 07

## Counterpower learning, implementation verification, and survivor-bias reconciliation

Wave 06 established that a visible correction can take several forms: evidence access, a hold, restoration, compensation, feature retirement, workflow revision, model or data revision, and follow-up. Wave 07 asks whether those effects became **institutional learning**.

The governing rule is:

```text
correction announced or ordered
≠ correction implemented
≠ worker repaired
≠ implementation independently verified
≠ recurrence controlled
≠ excluded and restored outcomes reconciled
```

This distinction follows the estate’s original selective-label mechanism. A selection-conditioned institution learns from the people it admits, retains, measures, and rewards while losing the outcomes of people it rejects, silences, deactivates, deters, or drives out. A remedy cannot close that epistemic loop unless those missing outcomes become observable and capable of changing the operative target.

## Outcome-population ledger

The audit refuses to validate a corrected system only on the people who remained. It separately tracks:

```text
workers measured or classified
workers adversely affected
workers who challenged or complained
workers restored or reactivated
workers compensated
workers who exited
workers who became silent or were deterred
external counterfactual outcomes
```

Silence, exit, deterrence, rejection, and deactivation remain missing evidence. They are not proof of consent, fit, safety, engagement, or organizational health.

## Interpretive law

```text
Correction is not learning.
Visibility is not authority.
Explanation is not restoration.
A regulatory fine is not worker compensation.
Restoration is not system revision.
A vendor statement is not independent verification.
Follow-up is not survivor-bias reconciliation.
```

An institution learns only when a correction reaches the operative design, survives independent verification and recurrence review, repairs the affected worker where applicable, and changes what the institution can observe about people it previously excluded.
