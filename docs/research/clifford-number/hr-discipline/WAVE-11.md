# Clifford Number HR accountability-consequence Wave 11

Wave 11 tests whether a severe employment or adjacent state process retains named authorship, the advice chain, independent welfare and conflict review, corrective authority, a consequence decision, worker or record repair, system correction, and recurrence escalation.

> Accountability requires named authorship, an owner with corrective power, a recorded consequence decision, worker or record repair where warranted, system correction, and recurrence escalation. Training alone is not accountability.

Missing public consequence evidence is neither proof of impunity nor proof of accountability. All controls remain unadopted, and the named cases retain their separate legal and causal domains.
