## Central mechanism

The selection genealogy classifies modern HR as a selection-conditioned prediction regime. Inside employment, the same problem becomes a survivor-conditioned management regime:

```text
institution defines normal work and acceptable voice
→ monitoring converts behavior and communication into signals
→ dissent, safety, accommodation or appeal may appear as deviation
→ compressed review turns deviation into adverse action
→ workers conform, become silent, leave or are removed
→ the remaining population looks more consistent
→ consistency is misreported as fit, safety or engagement
```

Wave 04 inserts counterpower before this loop can validate itself:

```text

→ worker and representative notice
→ context, safety and accommodation attachment
→ independent support and meaningful review
→ constrained interim action
→ explanation and challenge
→ restoration
→ model, threshold or workflow revision
→ validation that includes exits, silence and overturns
```

## What the public record supports

### Protected voice is not generic negativity

United States labor-law materials distinguish protected concerted activity from ordinary individual conduct and preserve representation rights in covered union settings. The EU Platform Work Directive goes further within its scope by prohibiting automated processing intended to predict exercise of association, collective-bargaining, collective-action, information or consultation rights and by protecting private exchanges with representatives.

Wave 04 does not classify any named worker's conduct. It creates a fail-closed state: where a signal may be protected, collective, safety-related or representative communication, unresolved status blocks adverse automation rather than becoming a risk feature.

### Safety exceptions must exist before the score

OSHA materials treat reporting and participation as protected safety infrastructure. California, New York and Minnesota make quotas, personal work-speed data, breaks, injury reduction and retaliation separately addressable. The control implication is that a pause for safety, injury, equipment failure or a required break cannot remain an unexplained productivity deviation.

### Accommodation is part of construct custody

EEOC guidance uses a keystroke metric to show how assistive technology can turn the same productive work into an apparently poor score. The relevant question is not whether everyone received the same metric. It is whether the metric measured the intended job capacity after accommodation and alternative evidence were considered.

### Collective transparency changes the time of intervention

Individual explanation usually arrives after a consequential output. Collective information, consultation and co-determination can operate before introduction or material change. Germany's Works Constitution Act, Spain's worker-representative algorithmic information right and the EU Platform Work Directive show different forms of that architecture. Their scopes and powers are not identical, but each refuses the idea that the worker must remain the only party outside the system definition.

### Some data should never become a management signal

The Platform Work Directive prohibits specified processing of private conversations, off-duty data, rights-exercise predictions and sensitive inferences. The EU AI Act's workplace emotion-recognition prohibition has applied since 2 February 2025, while the separate Annex III employment high-risk duties remain scheduled for 2 December 2027 on the current implementation timeline. Wave 04 refuses both temporal errors: deferring an operative prohibition and pretending future duties are already operative.

### Remedy must alter the system

Backpay, reinstatement, restored access, corrected records and explanation can repair immediate harm. They do not complete institutional learning unless the confirmed error changes the threshold, feature, workflow, training, vendor use or use case. A system that records only its retained workers and ignores successful challenges, exits, silence and reinstatements remains selection-conditioned.
