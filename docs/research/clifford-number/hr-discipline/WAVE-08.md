## Receipt states

1. `trigger_and_allegation_defined` — What event, complaint, allegation or concern initiated the process?
2. `severity_and_outcome_range_bounded` — Was practical job, liberty, family, reputation or status risk accurately bounded?
3. `notice_and_preparation_provided` — Was advance notice and a realistic preparation window provided?
4. `evidence_provenance_and_disclosure` — Were allegation provenance, corroboration and exculpatory evidence preserved and disclosed?
5. `companion_or_representation_available` — Could the person obtain an appropriate companion, representative or advocate?
6. `interim_restrictions_authored_and_reviewed` — Were restrictions named, proportionate, reviewed and marked non-prejudicial?
7. `independent_welfare_owner_assigned` — Was welfare owned outside the allegation, investigation and decision chain?
8. `distress_signal_assessed_and_escalated` — Did expressed or visible distress trigger a documented assessment?
9. `same_day_and_overnight_safety_handoff` — Was there a named same-day, overnight or next-day safety handoff?
10. `decision_authorship_and_conflicts_preserved` — Who owned classification, restrictions, support, escalation and final decisions?
11. `challenge_or_correction_channel_operational` — Was an effective challenge, appeal or correction path available in time?
12. `post_event_learning_independently_verified` — Did response become verified implementation, recurrence review and population learning?

## Boundary

Recovered absence means the public record supports non-performance of the state. `blocked` means the public record is insufficient. A cross-case pattern is a research synthesis, not a legal conclusion, universal prevalence claim, or finding of coordinated intent.
