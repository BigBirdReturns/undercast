The source genealogy classifies modern HR as a **selection-conditioned prediction regime**: institutions observe and reward survivors while losing evidence about people they exclude, discourage, silence or remove. Wave 03 carries that same selective-label problem inside employment. A deactivated worker, a rider who receives fewer orders, a worker who exits after metric pressure, or a person whose account is removed may disappear from the future validation population even though the decision system continues to learn from those who remain. The named records make portions of that loop observable without converting it into a universal-intent claim.

## The complete adverse-action chain

The matrix preserves fourteen states:

```text
use case registered
→ data sources fixed
→ protected-activity and safety rules fixed
→ construct, model, features and threshold fixed
→ alert generated
→ context attached
→ worker notified
→ support and welfare arranged
→ meaningful human review assigned
→ interim measures imposed
→ decision issued
→ evidence and explanation returned
→ challenge resolved
→ system outcome reconciled
```

A cell marked `recovered` means the public record makes that state addressable. It does **not** mean the state was lawful, accurate, fair, complete or equivalent across deployments. `partial` retains an incomplete join. `blocked` preserves the absence rather than filling it from institutional claims.

## Case corrections that cannot drift

The Amazon France record has two monetary states. The CNIL imposed EUR 32 million on 27 December 2023. The Conseil d’État partially reformed the decision on 23 December 2025 and reduced the final fine to EUR 15 million. The estate must retain both states and cannot report the initial amount as the final judicial result.

Seattle’s transition is equally exact. From 1 January 2025 through 31 May 2027, OLS may enforce specified deactivation procedures but not whether a deactivation reason was permissible. Merits enforcement begins 1 June 2027. Q4 2025 records nevertheless show concrete procedural remedies: process restart, reinstatement or reactivation and worker payments. Those remedies are not merits findings.

## Why this matters to the purge-capable loop

The six clusters expose different joins:

```text
scanner event → performance indicator → coaching or evaluation
peer comparison → undisclosed quota → pressure or discipline exposure
geolocation and acceptance → rating → work allocation or exclusion
route trace → assignment model → human correction duty
fraud probability → account removal → explanation order
opaque deactivation → procedural challenge → restart or reactivation
```

The central problem is not simply that a machine exists. It is whether the institution can name the purpose, data, model, threshold, context, reviewer, interim effect, final owner, explanation, challenge result and later correction. When those objects are split across vendors, managers, HR, security, lawyers and regulators, the worker experiences one coherent exertion while authorship fragments.

## What remains open

No cluster supplies a complete worker-level receipt across all fourteen states. Support and welfare custody is almost entirely absent. Context attachment, thresholds, false-positive rates, override history and survivor-conditioned model correction remain especially weak. Public remedies are real but uneven: fines, citations, explanation orders, process restarts and reactivations do not by themselves reveal the full foregone-income, health, reputation or opportunity cost.

## Hard refusals

Wave 03 refuses to infer:

- that every monitored or deactivated worker was wrongly classified;
- that every named company intended to eliminate dissent;
- that all algorithmic management is AI;
- that the six clusters establish universal prevalence;
- that a public fine proves every alleged fact or every individual causal chain;
- that a procedural remedy proves the original reason was impermissible;
- that Chloe Moffat’s process used AI;
- that victim character explains institutional harm;
- that an appeal or corrective order automatically reconciled the system against all missing outcomes.

## Interpretive law

Operational telemetry is not neutral merely because it is automatic. A functional quota does not cease to be a quota because it is called peer-to-peer evaluation. A rating is not merely feedback when it changes access to work. A probability score is not merely analytics when it removes an account or carries a fraud accusation. Human presence is not human review without time, context and reversal authority. A procedural restart is a remedy, but not a merits judgment. A fine is an accountability event, but not a complete worker receipt. Silence, exit and deactivation are missing labels, not proof that the system was right.
