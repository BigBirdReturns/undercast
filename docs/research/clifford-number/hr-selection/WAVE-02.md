## What the recovered spine supports

The strongest independently supported joins are now:

1. Merit reform was an authentic anti-patronage technology, and the Pendleton transaction joined examination to probation and written institutional records.
2. Scientific management and early industrial psychology placed work definition, selection, training, and output under management-authored classification.
3. Wartime group testing made psychological sorting operate at population scale even while construct and performance relations remained contested.
4. Professional assessment-center standards distinguish behavioral simulation from a computerized test battery; duration does not erase that difference.
5. *Griggs* and EEOC guidance created genuine job-relevance, adverse-impact, validation, and administration constraints, without supplying an individual false-negative audit.
6. A contextual competence critique was translated into reusable dictionaries, role profiles, norms, reports, and global vendor infrastructure.
7. Selective-label research supports the missing-outcome problem; exploration supplies one bounded empirical repair; performative prediction explains feedback; the 2026 monoculture study supplies cross-employer evidence in one vendor ecology.
8. Cultural matching, constructed criteria, meritocracy framing, and high-stakes personality response shifts provide bounded mechanisms for institutional self-reproduction without authorizing individual moral rankings.

None of those findings establishes that all measurement is illegitimate, that every assessment lacks predictive value, that every employer uses the same machinery, or that the private candidate transaction was unlawful.

## Draft Clifford Number protocol

`DECISION-RECEIPT-PROTOCOL.json` converts the source findings into an auditable transaction model while keeping all seven controls unadopted. It preserves burden across:

```text
job owner
→ HR
→ assessment vendor
→ hiring manager
→ employer
→ candidate
→ external auditor
```

The protocol requires explicit states for role definition, instrument selection, pretest accommodation disposition, assessment completion, signal-conflict resolution, decision issuance, candidate evidence return, and later prediction reconciliation.

Its exploration design is bounded rather than magical: pre-register a small uncertain or near-threshold sample, advance that sample to job-relevant simulation or richer structured review, retain accommodations and overrides, and compare later evidence against the original prediction. It creates neither automatic hiring nor retroactive entitlement.

## Interpretive law

An anti-patronage exam is not self-authenticating merit. A management-authored job model is not the whole job. A classification system’s scale is not proof of construct validity. A test battery is not a simulation. Job relation is not an individual false-negative audit. Vendor scale is not independent validation. Selected labels are not the full population.
