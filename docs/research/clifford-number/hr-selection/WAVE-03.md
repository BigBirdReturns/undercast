## What current governance actually recovers

### Federal accommodation and job-related use

The federal lane recovers an operative burden to make the application process accessible and to distinguish the intended job-related construct from disability-related interference. Alternative format or additional time can be necessary where speed is not itself the relevant skill. Official technical assistance also identifies algorithmic screen-out risk and the need for a usable accommodation process.

The Uniform Guidelines recover a different object: protected-group selection rates, adverse impact, job- and use-specific validation, and suitable less-adverse alternatives. The four-fifths rule is a practical indicator rather than an ultimate legal definition.

Neither object supplies an individual false-negative rate. Group impact does not tell the institution which rejected individual would have succeeded. Equal administration does not prove equal construct measurement.

### New York City AEDT custody

Local Law 144 creates an operative transaction for covered automated employment decision tools:

```text
classify the deployment
→ complete a recent independent bias audit
→ publish the audit summary
→ give advance notice
→ identify evaluated qualifications or characteristics
→ disclose data and retention information
→ provide instructions for an alternative-process or accommodation request
```

The employer or employment agency retains the compliance burden. A vendor-provided tool or audit does not become the statutory owner. The audit remains a group selection-rate object; it does not observe rejected candidates’ counterfactual performance or prove substantive role validity.

### European Union operator architecture

The AI Act’s binding text classifies employment recruitment, candidate selection, worker management, monitoring, and evaluation systems as high-risk categories. Its provider/deployer architecture preserves:

- provider documentation, instructions, quality management, and monitoring;
- deployer competence, training, authority, and meaningful human oversight;
- authority to disregard, override, or reverse output;
- workplace and affected-person notice;
- a clear and meaningful explanation for certain covered adverse decisions.

Those high-risk employment duties are not recorded as operative on the wave date. Current Commission implementation material places their application on **2 December 2027**. Other AI Act provisions have different dates and are outside this lane’s complete denominator.

### Colorado’s enacted future transaction

Colorado SB26-189 covers automated decision-making technology that materially influences consequential employment decisions. It separates developer and deployer burdens and schedules:

- developer documentation and material-change information;
- deployer notice;
- plain-language adverse-outcome explanation;
- access and correction;
- meaningful human review and reconsideration;
- three-year records.

Most substantive duties begin **1 January 2027**. The act does not create a new private right of action. Wave 03 therefore preserves the future control architecture without manufacturing present enforceability.

## Deployment custody protocol

`DEPLOYMENT-CUSTODY-PROTOCOL.json` turns distributed authorship into eight explicit states:

```text
role object fixed
→ provider and instrument fixed
→ configuration fixed
→ accommodation dispositioned
→ human oversight assigned
→ decision issued
→ candidate evidence returned
→ deployment reconciled
```

The protocol retains distinct burdens:

- **job owner:** essential work, operating conditions, success, and failure;
- **provider:** instrument, version, construct, norm, validation scope, and limitations;
- **HR/deployer:** role profile, features, weights, threshold, sequence, time burden, and proctoring;
- **accommodation owner:** request, intended construct, interactive process, disposition, and alternative method;
- **human reviewer:** competence, training, authority, override, and automation-bias control;
- **decision owner:** instrument evidence, human evidence, conflicts, controlling reason, and final exertion;
- **candidate-facing process:** intelligible notice, result, correction, appeal, and retention information;
- **audit owner:** selection rates, accommodations, overrides, drift, false-negative evidence, material changes, and revision.

The protocol does not compel hiring, expose protected trade secrets, or prove legal compliance. All seven controls remain `adopted: false`.

## Vendor boundary

The vendor lane keeps product documentation in its proper evidentiary class. A catalog can establish that a product offers simulations, behavioral measures, cognitive tests, and reports. Training materials can establish that a report describes **potential fit** and should be interpreted with other evidence. Accessibility guidance can document available adjustments.

Those materials do not reconstruct a named deployment’s:

- instrument version;
- norm group;
- role profile;
- feature set;
- weighting;
- threshold;
- sequence;
- time burden;
- proctoring;
- accommodation disposition;
- human override;
- final decision owner.

A quantified vendor false-negative claim remains a vendor claim until its denominator, comparison policy, role, data, and independent replication are recovered.

## False-negative audit protocol

The core epistemic defect is unchanged:

```text
selected candidate
→ receives work and opportunity
→ generates observable outcome

rejected candidate
→ receives no comparable opportunity
→ generates no employer outcome label
```

`FALSE-NEGATIVE-AUDIT-PROTOCOL.json` therefore refuses to infer missing outcomes. It specifies two bounded research stages.

### FN-A — rich-review exploration

Pre-register an uncertainty band and randomly sample within it. Give sampled candidates a paid, accessible, role-relevant simulation or structured review. Preserve accommodations, structured scoring, evidence conflicts, overrides, and reasons.

This stage observes whether richer evidence changes the initial classification. It does not prove eventual job success.

### FN-B — employment-outcome exploration

Where lawful, ethically reviewed, and organizationally authorized, observe a bounded uncertain cohort under comparable role assignment, onboarding, opportunity, support, compensation, and outcome horizons.

This stage is harder because opportunity is part of the treatment. A selector cannot give one cohort weaker assignments and then label the resulting performance difference as person quality.

The protocol retains denominators for the complete funnel, accommodations, standard and exploration advance, rejection, withdrawal, hiring, and observed outcomes. It records uncertainty rather than reporting a universal false-negative rate.

## Interpretive law

Operative law is not future law. Enactment is not application. Guidance is not adjudication. Vendor documentation is not deployment custody. Human presence is not oversight without competence and authority. Group impact is not an individual false-negative rate. A role profile is not the work. A score is not the decision owner. A report is not an explanation unless it identifies the relevant decision transaction. Exploration is not entitlement. Missing outcomes are not observed negatives. Later success is not retroactive hiring authority.
