# UnderCast estate product alignment

**Alignment checkpoint:** 2026-08-01  
**Consolidation venue:** `agent/estate-consolidation-001` / PR #132

## Product

UnderCast is a recognition engine for hidden human performance and making.

The primary interaction remains:

```text
recognize the designed character
→ flip the card
→ recognize the performer underneath
→ follow the makers and evidence when curiosity asks for more
```

The archive exists to make that recognition trustworthy. Archive machinery is not the product and may not displace visible estate improvement from the critical path.

## Product hierarchy

```text
recognition experience
    supported by evidence
        protected by machinery
```

A transaction is product-paying only when it does at least one of the following on the main-based consolidation line:

- adds or corrects a canonical character or performer image;
- resolves a filed absence or dispute honestly;
- improves a visitor-facing record, search path, maker credit, or correction history;
- integrates, deploys, or retires already-retained work.

Discovery runs, packets, receipts, branches, policies, supervisors, and workflow executions are inventory or operating cost until they produce one of those outcomes.

## Trust states for existing media

Every canonical media facet must be legible as one of three states:

1. **Frontier-certified** — new, repaired, disputed, or flagged media with exact source custody, exact subject and production binding, duplicate screening, independent review, deterministic crop proof, and a gated canonical-adoption receipt.
2. **Legacy-sourced** — a real sourced image that matches the filed record, passes current gross-mismatch and duplicate screens, and has no identified contradiction, but has not been reconstructed through the complete frontier packet process.
3. **Missing or contested** — no acceptable image is on file, or a contradiction remains unresolved. The product shows an explicit absence rather than a weak substitute.

The existing wall is not universally re-prosecuted. Complete frontier reconstruction is required by exception: for new media, known errors, disputes, weak provenance, source drift, duplicate alerts, wrong-adaptation risk, and other machine- or reviewer-flagged cases.

## Completion

A card or media facet is resolved when it has one of these explicit outcomes:

```text
verified image
verified absence
explicit unresolved dispute
inapplicable side
```

“Every slot contains an image” is not a completion definition. Fabricated or weakly bound completeness is a regression.

## Operating boundary

The durable control surface contracts toward three verbs:

```text
audit
adopt
release
```

Until the retained estate is integrated and visible:

- no new source-policy version merely retries an exhausted policy under another name;
- no new supervisor, packet format, branch family, or recovery workflow is added unless an indispensable product transaction cannot be expressed through the existing surface;
- packet production cannot outrank canonical adoption of already-reviewed packets;
- a workflow is temporary unless it enforces a continuing public-product invariant;
- every completed transaction names the workflows, branches, and controls that can now be retired;
- progress is reported in canonical records, resolved facets, deployed behavior, and retired debt—not raw run, commit, packet, or receipt counts.

## E-Hentai boundary

E-Hentai remains an operating reference for permanent identity, provenance, correction lineage, discovery, low-resource access, and continuity. It is not UnderCast’s product template, content policy, economy, community-authority model, or infrastructure target.

UnderCast adopts the smallest strong primitive that solves an observed archive failure. It does not build mature-looking infrastructure in anticipation of scale.
